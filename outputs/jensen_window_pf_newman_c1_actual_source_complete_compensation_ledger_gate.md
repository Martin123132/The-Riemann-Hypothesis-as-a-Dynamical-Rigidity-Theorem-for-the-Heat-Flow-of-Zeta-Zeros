## Compensation test

At B<-49/100, negativity requires E_tot<-(98/100)rho A_T.

At the same adverse witness, E_tot>=-(98/100)rho A_T implies Sigma_full>0 and therefore a complete-current sign change.

If E_tot=E_F+E_sec and |E_sec|<=delta rho A_T, then negativity at the adverse witness requires E_F<-(98/100-delta)rho A_T.

A proof may establish a sufficiently strong cell-uniform negative bias or a pointwise phase-correlated inequality; the adverse threshold alone does not choose between them.
