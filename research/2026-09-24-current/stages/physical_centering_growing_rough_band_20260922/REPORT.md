## New written results

1. **A fixed-power Euler tail.** The ORIGINAL prime-count cap implies
   prod_H(1-p^(-1/6))^-1 <= d^2. Thus every first/third Euler angular
   character has a double-series tail <=2 d^6 X^(-1/3). The prime schedule
   is unchanged; allowing this polynomial d cost improves the N scale.

2. **A growing rough-frequency band with a power-saving error.** For reduced
   v=r/s with r,s<=N^eta, prime support disjoint from H, eta<2/3, and ANY
   integer Gamma index, the actual-height centered character has error
   O(d^6 N^(-(2/3-eta)/4)), apart from explicit pure-Gamma terms. This is
   uniform in the two original windows. For eta=1/6 a coefficient budget
   N^(1/12) costs O(d^6 N^(-1/24))=o(1). Optimizing a fixed Rankin exponent
   below 1/2 admits every eta<4/15 with a sqrt(N^eta) coefficient budget.
   These are asymptotic statements, not small-height numerical margins.

3. **The exceptional phase coefficients have sharp product scales.** The
   first coefficient is comparable to Z_H^(-1/4), and the third to
   Z_H^(-9/4). Their renormalized products converge to positive constants
   along the original complete prefix. No effective N-rate is asserted.
   The five first-mode and five leading third-mode pure-Gamma terms are
   recorded explicitly with their SIGNS; they are not silently set to zero.

4. **An explicit approximation-residual criterion for the FULL response.**
   For any admitted finite response polynomial, the centered current equals
   its finite signed Gamma contribution, plus its actual centered residual,
   plus the paid spectral error. A residual L1 bound is sufficient, but
   keeping its sign may be stronger. The exact restricted cubic, global
   multipliers and source-dependent inverse amplitude all remain attached.

## What remains

We have NOT proved that the actual nonlinear responses possess an inexpensive
rough-frequency approximation with a small residual. Their inverse-Euler
expansion contains shifted symbols, H-overlap and top-index terms; nonlinear
composition can reintroduce long frequency ratios. Neither centering nor
vanishing first moment removes rare quadratic energy.

The next source-dependent task is to construct/pay that approximation, or
bound its remaining signed correlation directly, on both original windows
and the full profile. The sufficient limit remains 0.00092027989; no new
favourable signed threshold, zero count, or RH conclusion is obtained here.
All proof-wide restoration obligations and the 0.0011250999 ledger remain.
