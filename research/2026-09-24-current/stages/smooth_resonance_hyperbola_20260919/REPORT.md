## Result

first-mode, zero-carrier near-resonant sector. It does not prove the complete

    n >= ceil(N/sqrt(d)), |buv-an| <= floor(sqrt(d)),

## Why it works

On a fixed offset h=buv-an, split the ordered factor pairs at v<=u and
retain the diagonal correction. For each smaller factor v, the allowed
larger factors lie in fixed residue classes modulo 30a. The actual phase
has an explicit second derivative of constant sign. The classical
second-derivative exponential-sum estimate, followed by partial summation,
bounds their collective signed sum before the common inverse is applied.

Both signs of h are included. The exact offset h=0 is impossible for a
nonconstant reduced smooth ratio. For the constant ratio, its surviving
uv=n diagonal is separately paid, not dismissed as oscillatory.

The proof uses the classical estimate reproduced on page 1, equation (1),
of Heath-Brown's paper linked in SOURCES.md. The new work is its application
to this constrained, weighted factorization sum with the shared denominator.

The earlier power-width thin-band result and this new logarithmic-width,
broader-denominator result are complementary. Neither covers all product
ratios. Their intersection must not be charged twice when used together.
