## The new arithmetic step

We can remove the RATIO contribution on a thin neighborhood of any fixed
rational ratio b/a, despite its dependence on the complete arithmetic source.
The mechanism is not independence:

1. In a tube |log(n/((b/a)m))|<=N^-2/3, replacing its omitted-background
   coefficient by the FULL-source coefficient has an explicit small absolute
   cost O(d^-5/2). The actual full source still varies with height.
2. The remaining nonzero-offset reciprocal-phase kernel has a POINTWISE
   power-saving bound from the inherited exponent-pair interface.
3. Its product with that bounded full-source coefficient is therefore small
   without treating either factor as independent or slowly varying.

The exact rational diagonal is retained and paid separately: O(1/d). It is
absent when the ratio forces an excluded prime into an S-rough index.
With that diagonal absent, the complete tube price is

    O(d^-5/2+N^-1/186/d^2).

The bare exponent calculation is -1/93. The statement deliberately reserves
a small positive exponent loss and uses -1/186. Constants depend on the
FIXED numerator, denominator, profile compact and prime set. There is no
uniform theorem for a growing family of rationals.

This new arithmetic removal is conditional on the same perturbed-reciprocal
partial-sum interface used in the authenticated first-moment proof. That
dependency is stated in full in TUBE_DERIVATION.md. Finite tests do not prove
the interface or the full RH argument.

-At width delta=N^-3/5, the ratio contribution near n/m=1 costs
 O(N^-1/5/d^2+N^-3/5/d), using its actual log(n/m)/d factor.
-Product contributions near any fixed multiple of A=N+(1+2nu)/4 cost
 O(N^-1/20/d^2), using the q=1/2 residual factor and integer divisor grouping.
-The complete-pair contribution from mn>=N^2 exp(-delta) costs O(N^-1/5/d).

These are explicit polynomial-width deletions. The previous stage supplied
only a slowly expanding, noneffective whole-pair cutoff. The distinction
matters: the first two new statements remove COMPONENTS, not every term
belonging to those pairs. The tests enforce this bookkeeping.

## What remains

ratios whose source-dependent interaction is not bounded by these lemmas.

slowly varying. A physical-height integration that drops its derivatives
would still be invalid; the old fully restored K integration remains circular.
