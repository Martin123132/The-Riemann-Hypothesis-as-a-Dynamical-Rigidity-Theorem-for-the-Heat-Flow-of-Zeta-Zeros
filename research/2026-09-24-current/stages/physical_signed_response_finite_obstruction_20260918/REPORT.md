## What it does not establish

This certificate concerns the finite HOMOGENEOUS DIRICHLET MODEL from the
preceding compact reduction. It is not a finite bound for the full auxiliary
function. The replacement by that model was proved asymptotically on fixed
compact x intervals, not with an effective finite-N onset.

The interval x=[1/8,16] is fixed. Neither its small-x complement nor its
large-x complement is silently set to zero. The global theorem still needs
the ordered compact/end limits and all inherited payments.

Thus the result refutes a sign-preserving free-angle substitution on every
window. It does NOT refute eventual asymptotic cancellation, the remaining
signed limsup target, or RH. Nor does the successful finite total certify
that target on an unbounded family.
