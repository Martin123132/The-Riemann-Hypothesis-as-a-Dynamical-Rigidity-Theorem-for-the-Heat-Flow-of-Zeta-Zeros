3. Exact division of the actual source by that Euler product has a controlled

4. The difference between that comparison and the actual signed response is
   an explicit centered finite-prime correlation. It has a paid finite Fourier
   approximation, but its required signed bound remains open.

## The transfer test prevents a false conclusion

An explicit control satisfies the entire external-prime energy law, a flat
Euler-divided energy law, zero conditional linear mean and quadratic isotropy.
Its signed response is nevertheless beta times its energy. Under the
authenticated energy floor that is >0.00761075, whereas our target is
0.00092128989. For primes 2,3,5 its dependence defect is >0.00750.

This is not a counterexample to RH or to the actual source. It demonstrates
that prime-energy statistics alone cannot justify replacing the shared-height
source by independent prime averaging. Rare-amplitude concentration preserves
the counterexample, so deleting low-probability events does not resolve it.
