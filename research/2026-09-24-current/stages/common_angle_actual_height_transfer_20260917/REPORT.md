## A route that still avoids switch counting

The new angular-primitive bound gives, for monotone positive phase speed,

    |Lambda| <= (2*B_H/(window_length*lambda)+D_H/lambda)/barW,

where H is the zero-mean angular primitive, lambda bounds phase speed
below, B_H bounds H and D_H bounds its physical-variable derivative while
the auxiliary angle is held fixed. Uniform bounds for a maximum of
Lipschitz candidates do not count changes of the active candidate.

The exact integration-by-parts identity also retains the SIGNED derivative
term. That term is the useful next arithmetic target. The generic absolute
norm estimate may be too large: no small source-specific Lambda is proved.
