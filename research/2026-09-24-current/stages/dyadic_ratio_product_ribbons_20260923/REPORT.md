## Mechanism

Complete dyadic rectangles allow their signed explicit factors to contract
into two block sums before taking absolute values. The four-corner identity
then prices the actual pair-omitted background without making it independent
or differentiating it in physical height. A common logarithmic recentering
preserves the ratio difference. Complementary recentering preserves the
different product sum. The nonzero product diagonal is explicitly paid.

The uniform physical block-energy estimate is proved with a smooth majorant
of the original window and the partial-interval reciprocal estimate. It is
not inferred from a fixed-symbol limit across a growing family. The finite
tests below cannot prove that unbounded analytic estimate by sampling.

This is an ALTERNATIVE direct reduction from J2. It must not be combined with
the prior signed tube deletion by merely adding their prices: their overlap
would need its own bound. In particular arbitrary masks inside the complete
rectangles are not licensed. The larger O(d^-1/2) price and the incoming
O(d^-7/8) price refer to different removed sets.
