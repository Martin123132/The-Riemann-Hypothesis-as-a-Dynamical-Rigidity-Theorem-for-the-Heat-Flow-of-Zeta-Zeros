## New actual-source result

A single rotating carrier cannot make the original source nearly constant
over the filter shifts. Uniformly over every height-independent carrier
gamma (even depending on N and profile),

    integral k_delta(v) integral
       |e^(i gamma v)P(t+v)-P(t)|^2 dnu dv -> 2E_0 > 0.

The original coefficient energy is E_0. A whole-cell profile enclosure,
rechecked from exact rational endpoints, gives E_0>0.0021. Thus the limiting
increment energy exceeds 0.0042. These are NOT signed-current margins or
effective finite-height bounds.

The arithmetic input is a uniform positive bound for narrow linear
frequency bands. More generally, a bounded, controlled-variation mask of
any fixed power of log(N) such bands captures only O(log(log N)/log N)
source energy. It cannot approximate the full linear source in this norm.
That statement does not apply to the nonlinear phase or arbitrary fitted
models.

This settles one question negatively: the source-increment Cauchy bound
cannot become small just by choosing a better single carrier. It does not
show that the actual signed phase contribution is adverse.

## A signed expression to attack

The two modes, every gate harmonic, and the selected-character multiplier
can be combined exactly into

    |P(t+v)|^2 W(Phi) * [(1-c) Re(Z1+Z3) + e Im(Z1-Z3)],

where c+i e=U(t+v)conjugate(U(t)), Phi=chi(t)+v log A,
Z1=alpha M1(t+v)e^(i Phi)U(t+v), and
Z3=beta M3(t+v)e^(3i Phi)U(t+v)^3.

This has no division by the source, no separate worst-case mode signs,
and no missing global subtraction. DERIVATION.md also gives a symmetric
two-height form retaining the exact endpoint density. The odd increment
does not cancel automatically: its coefficient difference is odd too.

The polynomial's exact atom is absent by parity for admitted large N;
the nonlinear exact atom is NOT absent and retains its original reserve.
The remaining one-sided signed threshold is still
0.000920279889999993. It is NOT proved in this package.
