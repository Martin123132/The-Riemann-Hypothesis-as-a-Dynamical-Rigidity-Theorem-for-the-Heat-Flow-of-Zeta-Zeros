## A necessary second rational approximation

The new bridge handoff's algebra is valid, but its final smoothness claim
needs one correction: min(C,Y) still has a corner after smoothing only the
transverse minimum. We keep all historical scores unchanged and approximate
|C-Y| as well as |X|. With a compatible smooth lower comparison ell,

    Hhat=(C+Y-A_D(C-Y))/2-ell,
    That=E[X Hhat/2+A_X(X)(Hhat-Y)/2].

Both approximation errors are paid. The actual-minimum source-wide error
is <20/sqrt N; a lower ell retains its own extra payment. The two factored
sums have O(log^2 N) nodes each, but expanding their product can generate
O(log^4 N) paired terms. No approximate gate is treated as a probability.
See TWO_GATE_DERIVATION.md.
