## New continuation

The incoming proof has more exponential saving than its original offset
roster spends. Define

    d=ceil(log(N+1)), ell=bit_length(d), m=ceil(sqrt ell),
    G=3d/[16m(2^(16m)-2)],
    H_new=2^max(floor(sqrt d),floor(G/2)).

With the SAME enlarged core and fixed finite character roster, all selected
nonzero offsets 1<=|buv-an|<=H_new have total inverse-weighted cost

    O(d^(3/2) exp(-G/2) + d^(-1/2) exp(-d/(8m))) = o(1).

This includes all retained core indices and all five first-mode carriers.
The proof rechecks the derivative-order, denominator-separation, coefficient
variation and uniform-constant requirements with the larger offset cap.

The new cap never shrinks the old one; asymptotically

    log(H_new)/log(H_old) -> infinity,   H_new=N^o(1).

This is a wider proved ASYMPTOTIC range, not a practical finite-height
improvement. No effective source onset or new physical source evaluation is
claimed. The parameter-only threshold m>=128 is not such an onset.

Complete core transfer must come before polynomial division. Only then may
the selected band be subtracted from the short and high expressions with
its common inverse weights. Dividing an arbitrarily selected cubic subset
as though it were the complete cubic would be invalid. DERIVATION.md gives
the exact accounting and the equivalent older amplitude-partition route.
