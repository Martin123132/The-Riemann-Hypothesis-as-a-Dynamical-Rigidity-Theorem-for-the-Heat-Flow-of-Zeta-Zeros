## Analytic review

1. The new central-energy input is unconditional. Page 1 of Bourgain and Watt,
   arXiv:1505.04161v1, explicitly records Watt's bound with exponent 131/416
   and logarithmic exponent 32587/8320 < 4. The original window length is
   comparable to N^(2/3), while the global error is O(N^(131/208) log^4 N).
   Their exponent gap is 23/624. The source-to-Hardy bridge retains the removed
   terms, complete auxiliary remainder, Gamma phase error and deterministic shift.
   This supports O(sqrt(log N)) central L2 control, not an effective finite bound.
2. The uniform mixed-coefficient limit retains the full central polynomial.
   Only the upper coefficient is shortened, with the L2 error paid before the
   limit. Near-cutoff products are included. Spectral passage uses fixed finite
   support first and a summable majorant; it does not assume uniform convergence
   over a growing Dirichlet support.
3. The gap inequality, lower-block screening, selection covariance and switching
   primitive identity are exact finite-dimensional/integral identities. A small
   primitive does not imply a small switching sum. The supplied counterexample
   correctly rejects that inference.
4. The phase-reference calculation is a distinct rule. Its Fejer degree grows;
   the L2 mask error is paid against the actual central factor. The change to the
   actual phase variable retains the positive Jacobian. Its harmonics introduce
   no prime characters. The two constant resonances give the stated mask formula;
   the common diagonal cancels in the covariance. Conjugate ratio terms can be
   conjugated before applying the stated positive-ratio phase classification.
5. The limit near 1.44612 applies only to an ordinary selected correlation for
   that phase-only reference. It is not a finite N=64 lower bound, not the old
   eight-term argmin, and not a higher allowance for the old R-Q expression.

The written analytic arguments have been reviewed under the inherited kernel and
source estimates. These are not machine-formalized proofs, nor a fresh proof of
